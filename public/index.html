A LOOK UNDER THE HOOD

Practice with purpose.

InterviewReady tailors questions to your target role and gives written feedback using four transparent criteria: relevance, structure, specificity, and clarity.

Three prompt engineering patterns

  1. Persona: a constructive interviewer, with clear rules for evidence and scoring.
  2. Few-shot: examples of weak and strong answers calibrate feedback.
  3. Iterative refinement: revise your answer and the coach compares it with your previous attempt.

Your final score uses your latest attempt for each answered question. These are practice scores, not hiring predictions.

Live answers and job descriptions are sent to Groq. Sessions stay in this server’s memory and disappear on restart or after 12 idle hours. The browser remembers the session ID and unsent draft for refresh recovery. Nothing is published.

Sample mode uses fixed example questions and feedback without calling Groq; it does not assess your answer.
